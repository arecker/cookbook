# S'mores Bars

Author: Marissa Recker

Graham cracker cookie bars with chocolate and marshmallow fluff. No campfire needed. Makes one 13x9-inch pan (about 36 bars).

## Materials

- flour, all purpose (2 1/2 cups)
- graham cracker crumbs (1 1/2 cups)
- baking soda (1/2 tsp)
- table salt (1/2 tsp)
- butter, unsalted, softened (1 cup or 2 sticks)
- brown sugar, light (3/4 cup)
- white sugar (3/4 cup)
- eggs (2)
- vanilla extract (2 tsp)
- regular size chocolate bars, such as Hershey's (9 bars, about 1.5 oz each)
- marshmallow fluff (1 tub or 16 oz)
- shortening or nonstick spray, for the pan

## Procedure

1. Pre-heat the oven to 350F. Grease a 13x9-inch baking dish.

2. In a bowl, mix the flour, graham cracker crumbs, baking soda, and salt.

3. In a large bowl, cream the butter and both sugars until light and fluffy. Mix in the eggs and vanilla, then mix in the flour mixture.

4. Press about half the dough into the bottom of the dish in an even layer. Grease your hands if the dough sticks.[^1]

5. Lay the chocolate bars in one layer over the dough. Break pieces to fill the gaps along the edges.

6. Spread the marshmallow fluff over the chocolate.[^2]

7. Cover the fluff with the rest of the dough. Either flatten small pieces in your hands and lay them on top, or roll the dough into a 13x9 rectangle on parchment, flip it onto the fluff, and peel off the parchment. Fix any holes with your fingers. Gaps are fine.

8. Bake 25-30 minutes, until golden brown around the edges.

9. Cool completely before cutting into squares. The chocolate and fluff stay soft and gooey while warm.

[^1]: the dough is sticky. Use a little butter on your fingers or the back of a fork to spread it.
[^2]: do NOT use regular marshmallows. They melt into a mess. Use marshmallow fluff only.
