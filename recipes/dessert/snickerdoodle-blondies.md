# Snickerdoodle Blondies

Author: Marissa Recker

Chewy blondie bars with white chocolate and a cinnamon-sugar swirl. Makes 16-20 bars.

## Materials

- butter, unsalted, softened (3/4 cup or 170g)
- white sugar (3/4 cup or 150g), plus 1/4 cup (50g) for cinnamon-sugar
- brown sugar, light, packed (3/4 cup or 150g)
- eggs, room temperature (2)
- vanilla extract (2 tsp)
- flour, all purpose (2 1/3 cups or 291g), plus extra for your hands
- baking powder (1 1/2 tsp)
- cream of tartar (1/2 tsp)
- table salt (1/2 tsp)
- white chocolate morsels (1 cup or 180g, plus 1 Tbsp for topping)
- cinnamon, ground (2 tsp)

## Procedure

1. Pre-heat the oven to 350F. Line a 9-inch square pan with parchment, leaving overhang on two sides.

2. Cream the butter, white sugar, and brown sugar on medium-high until fluffy and light, about 3 minutes. Add the eggs and vanilla and beat until combined. Scrape the bowl.

3. In a separate bowl, whisk the flour, baking powder, cream of tartar, and salt. Add to the wet mixture and beat on low until combined. Mix in 1 cup of white chocolate morsels. The dough will be thick and sticky.

4. Flour your hands. Press half the dough into an even layer in the pan.[^1]

5. Mix the 1/4 cup sugar and cinnamon. Sprinkle over the bottom layer, saving 1 tsp for the top.

6. Flour your hands again. Flatten small handfuls of the remaining dough and lay them over the cinnamon layer like puzzle pieces. Some cinnamon-sugar showing through is fine.

7. Press the last tablespoon of white chocolate morsels into the top. Sprinkle with the reserved cinnamon-sugar.

8. Bake 32-35 minutes, until golden brown and a toothpick comes out mostly clean. Do not over-bake.

9. Cool completely in the pan on a rack, about 1 hour. Lift out with the parchment and cut into squares.

10. Store covered at room temperature for up to 1 week. Freezes up to 3 months.

[^1]: the dough is very sticky. Re-flour your hands as often as you need to.
